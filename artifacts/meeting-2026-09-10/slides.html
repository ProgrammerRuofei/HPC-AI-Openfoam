Conclusion

Current Conclusion & Next Step

Established

  • strong scaling is already good
  • decomposition headroom is small
  • MPI placement is reasonable
  • ICC generic is not faster
  • architecture targeting adds SIMD
  • memory-hierarchy stalls are substantial

Unknown

  • MPI wall-time fraction
  • exact memory bandwidth saturation
  • cross-socket / cross-NUMA variation
  • whether a fixed AVX512 build would be faster end-to-end

Next

  • Memory / cache behaviour before further compiler tuning.
  • Remaining allocation is low — preserve evidence and resume runtime profiling after allocation reset.
  • ICC architecture targeting: paused, artifacts and provenance retained.
