# Four-node ICX 2026.2 / Intel MPI 2021.19 candidate v2

The original four-node job `182194314.gadi-pbs` finished Exit Status 143
after Scotch decomposition and before the solver. Its rank-map failed in
Intel MPI `MPI_Init` and charged 157.62 SU. Later two-node, two-rank
preflight `182237093.gadi-pbs` proved the private libfabric 2.6.13
environment was missing from the launcher and that sourcing its official
`env/vars.sh` allows default OFI provider `mlx` to complete MPI_Init,
Sendrecv and Allreduce across two SPR nodes. That job cost 6.59 SU.

The user approved one revised four-node run after reviewing the proposed
diff. v2 retains the original private toolchain and OpenFOAM archives,
scientific source SHA-256 list, mesh, simpleFoam binary, fvSchemes,
fvSolution, Scotch decomposition, 416 ranks on four normalsr SPR nodes,
and 150 SIMPLE steps. It sources private libfabric, verifies its SONAME
and `fi_info`, disables Intel MPI placement inheritance, and names the four
allocated PBS hosts explicitly. The existing rank-map gate moves before
copying and decomposing the 65M-cell case; a startup failure should therefore
stop before that expensive preparation. PBS resources remain 416 CPUs,
760 GB memory, 100 GB JOBFS per node and one hour (request ceiling
approximately 832 SU). New stdout and job-keyed run paths preserve v1.
No automatic repeat or further four-node experiment is authorized.
